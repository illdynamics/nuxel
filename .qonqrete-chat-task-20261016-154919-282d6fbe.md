## Touchscreen

A32 discovered a major hardware-specific condition.

When the Samsung boots with:

`androidboot.mode=charger`

the kernel sets:

`lpcharge=1`

and the `sec_ts` touchscreen driver deliberately refuses initialization.

In this mode, the touchscreen may not exist as an input device at all.

A32 demonstrated that a normal OS reboot removed that charger-mode condition and allowed `sec_touchscreen` to register.

It also demonstrated libinput discovery of the touchscreen.

**Physical finger-to-application interaction remains unproven in the supplied release report.**

Do not rewrite the driver until the present boot's mode and input-event path have been checked.

## Buttons

- Short Power press screen toggling.

- Configuration through `nuxelctl` and Settings.
- Status-bar safe-area support.

## Audio

The system sees:

`beyond-madera`

but actual speaker playback has not been demonstrated.

A32's mixer control success does not prove sound output.

## Important firmware constraint

The S10+ repository documents the Samsung Shannon LTE modem as not yet supported through an upstream ModemManager plugin.

Do not confuse this with the separate Pixel 10 Pro XL modem documentation.

Never modify radio calibration or the modem firmware partition.
