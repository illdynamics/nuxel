Generative backend

llama.cpp and ONNX Runtime archives can be installed above. This build also ships a built-in local responder so conversation, routing, device status and voice work immediately without a Nuxel host.

Voice · on-device speech

Android speech recognition uses the phone's speech service. The microphone closes as soon as speech ends, the transcript is placed in the message field and sent automatically. PenGuru never sends audio to a Nuxel host; depending on the phone's recognizer, Android may process speech online. MiniLM routing is optional; the built-in rules work without it.

Microphone closed

With auto-send off, tap the conversation mic to start and tap it again to send. The same setting is available on the conversation page. Foreground dictation stops when you hide the app or turn PenGuru off. Speech never authorizes privileged actions.

Talkback · spoken answers

Talkback reads PenGuru's answers aloud using an installed Android TextToSpeech engine. It works immediately with the phone's system voices. Spark-TTS and Kokoro voice packages are in the Model library as pinned, verified downloads for the on-device neural talkback runtime.

SonarBird background listening

Keep PenGuru on duty after you leave the app. A persistent notification shows that the microphone is in use; say “Penguru” followed by a question and it is sent to the conversation automatically. The phone's Android speech service handles the audio (it may use the network); PenGuru does not send it to a Nuxel host. Android may stop the listener when battery saver is aggressive — the battery exemption below prevents that.

Background listening is off.

Background reliability

PenGuru checks these permissions every time it opens. The battery exemption keeps Android from stopping the microphone notification and model downloads when the screen is off. All files access lets a reinstalled APK find Download/PenGuru instead of downloading every model and runtime again.

Privacy

Prompts and answers stay in this app's memory for the session. Model files and runtime archives are stored in this app's Android data directory. No cloud inference or telemetry. Turning PenGuru off stops downloads and workers.
