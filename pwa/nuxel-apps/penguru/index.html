Starting the on-device engine…

PenGuru is a virtual penguin: the most advanced AI subsystem of NUXAI, originally designed for Nuxel and now running on this Android phone. No Nuxel server, API URL or token is required. Voice transcripts are sent automatically; saying “Penguru” first works in every mic mode and strips the wake word. Say “Penguru chat” or “Penguru code” to force ChatBird or CodeBird. Generated code is never run automatically.

Tap the mic to talk; tap it again to stop, transcribe and send.

Voice · on-device speech

When the Whisper tiny.en model is installed, the Conversation mic records with PenGuru's own microphone from the first tap and transcribes on this phone. The phone's speech recognizer remains selectable in Settings and is used when Whisper is not ready. The microphone opens immediately, keeps recording until you tap again, and a long recording is not discarded while it is being transcribed. The default conversation behaviour is tap to talk, tap again to stop, transcribe and send. PenGuru never sends audio to a Nuxel host; if you explicitly select an online Android recognizer, Android may process that speech online.

Microphone closed

"Speak a message" always keeps the microphone open for 5 seconds, then transcribes and sends everything you said in that window. The conversation mic follows the mode above. Foreground dictation stops when you hide the app or turn PenGuru off. Speech never authorizes privileged actions.

Voice activity, commands & noise

Checking adaptive VAD, noise suppression and optional Silero / RNNoise packages…

The always-available endpointing is an adaptive RMS voice-activity detector. Optional Silero VAD and RNNoise packages are listed in the Model library; Android's built-in NoiseSuppressor is used automatically when present. Whisper tiny.en is the offline fallback recognizer and is listed both under On-device dependencies (bundled engine) and Model library (downloadable model).

Talkback · spoken answers

Talkback reads PenGuru's answers aloud using an installed Android TextToSpeech engine. While SonarBird background listening is running, the foreground service owns the voice engine, so answers are still spoken with the screen off or PenGuru behind another app. It works immediately with the phone's system voices. If the phone exposes Piper or Spark-TTS as an Android TTS engine, it appears in the engine list below. The pinned Spark-TTS and Kokoro packages are also in the Model library for the on-device neural voice runtime.

SonarBird background listening

Keep PenGuru on duty after you leave the app. A persistent notification shows that the microphone is in use; say “Penguru” and then your sentence. The notification shows the partial transcript live and the sentence is finalised as soon as the adaptive VAD detects that you stopped talking. The completed answer is spoken by the foreground service through talkback, and the microphone re-arms after it finishes so you can reply without repeating the wake word. Say “Penguru stop” (or tap the notification action) to stop the listener, “Penguru send” to send the sentence captured so far, and “Penguru chat” / “Penguru code” to force ChatBird or CodeBird for the next request. If the phone has no Android speech service, SonarBird falls back to the bundled Whisper tiny.en model when it is installed. The phone's Android speech service handles the audio (it may use the network); PenGuru does not send it to a Nuxel host. Android may stop the listener when battery saver is aggressive — the battery exemption below prevents that.

Background listening is off.

Background reliability

PenGuru checks these permissions every time it opens. The battery exemption keeps Android from stopping the microphone notification and model downloads when the screen is off. All files access lets a reinstalled APK find Download/PenGuru instead of downloading every model and runtime again.

Privacy

Prompts and answers stay in this app's memory for the session. Model files and runtime archives are stored in this app's Android data directory. No cloud inference or telemetry. Turning PenGuru off stops downloads and workers.
