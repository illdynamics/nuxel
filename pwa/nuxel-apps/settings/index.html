Boot diagnostics

Three independent controls, one durable document (/etc/nuxel/boot/verbose-boot.json). All three apply from the next boot, are written by nuxelctl verbose-boot … and by this panel through the same file, and are never overwritten by a package/rootfs update. Precedence for the animation is off > short > verbose boot > standard; the verbosity level only picks how much the log says, never whether it is shown.
