Where the sound would go

Read from the running audio stack through the bridge (audio.route). If the bridge does not answer, every field says “not reported” rather than guessing — and the expected route for this device (ALSA beyond-madera on the Exynos 9820) is documented, not invented.

What this build claims, and what it does not

  • Claims: a real <audio>/<video> element decodes and plays the bundled reference tones; transport, seek, queue, repeat and shuffle operate on real media time.
  • Claims: volume requests go to the real mixer through audio.setVolume and the displayed value is the mixer's own echo.
  • Does NOT claim: that anything was audible. A successful play() proves the media pipeline accepted the stream, not that a speaker moved air.
  • Does NOT claim: hardware codecs. Only what canPlayType reports is listed.
  • Does NOT claim: a device library scan when the badge says sample.

Owner listening test (manual proof area 41)

  1. Prepare: phone on Nuxel, at the launcher, sound you are allowed to make.
  2. Open Media → Player → tap ▶︎ on “Reference tone — A4 440 Hz”.
  3. Listen to the speaker. Then tap ⏭ to play “Reference tone — A5 880 Hz” and listen again.
  4. Expected: two clearly different steady tones; the seek bar advances; “state: playing”.
  5. Failure symptoms: silence with the bar advancing (route/decode problem), an immediate “Playback failed” message (decoder or device problem), or a stuck bar (transport problem).
  6. Record the result in docs/beta-finalization/BETA-MANUAL-PROOF-CHECKLIST.md area 41.

Diagnostics: journalctl -u nuxel-media* --no-pager | tail -40 · aplay -l · wpctl status · cat /proc/asound/cards.

Until that test is recorded, this app reports audibility as UNVERIFIED — a passing VM smoke test never yields a hardware verdict.
