Speed changes apply to this round. Use − / + beside the board while playing.

🐍 HOW TO PLAY

Swipe on the board, press the D-pad, or use your keyboard's arrow/WASD keys. Eat red food to grow. Avoid your body and poop piles.

Walls wrap around — leaving one side enters the opposite side. Classic Walls is available in Settings.

Poop-o-meter: In WoNQ Mode, every fifth food drops a persistent brown obstacle at the tail. A collision ends the game.

Controls: Space pauses, Escape/Back pauses or closes an open dialog. Locking the phone or leaving the app automatically pauses the game.
