## Mission in one sentence

## Already-diagnosed ground truth

- XRDP failed because the `::1:5901` loopback mirror was dead after the
  compositor restarted, the `LoadCredential` xrdp.ini was stale, and libvnc's
  local PAM check failed (stale owner password).
- VNC dropped because neatvnc mis-framed Jump Desktop's **Apple DH** response.
- Fixes are in `libneatvnc.so.1.0.4`, `nuxel-remote-desktop-ctl`, the loopback
  drop-in, and the `nuxel` account password. See §2 of the full prompt.
