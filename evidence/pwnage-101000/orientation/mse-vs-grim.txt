Orientation validation: raw screencopy buffer rotated in the bridge vs grim
(grim applies the compositor wl_output transform to the same capture).

Each orientation was captured in one controlled run (bridge stopped, grim +
bridge --once with --dump-output), then the converted 1920x1080 output was
cropped to its aspect-preserving content box, resized to grim's dimensions,
and compared pixel-for-pixel.

  effective=0   transform=0   (portrait):          MSE 160.42 mean diff 5.634
  effective=90  transform=270 (landscape):         MSE  53.36 mean diff 2.917
  effective=270 transform=90  (reverse landscape): MSE  53.36 mean diff 2.917
  effective=180 transform=180 (reverse portrait):  MSE 150.70 mean diff 5.584

The centre pixel matched exactly in every case:
  portrait:          grim (21,60,89)   output (21,60,89)
  landscape:         grim (20,54,83)   output (20,54,83)
  reverse landscape: grim (20,54,83)   output (20,54,83)
  reverse portrait:  grim (21,60,89)   output (21,60,89)

The residual MSE is bilinear resampling from the letterboxed 1920x1080
content box back to grim's logical size, not a rotation/colour error.

Key measurement that fixed the sign:
  raw 720x1520 rotated clockwise 270 == grim 1520x720 exactly (MSE 0.0)
  at effective=90 / transform=270.  The bridge therefore applies the reported
  transform value, not its inverse.

Live transition sequence (same bridge PID 226894 throughout):
  portrait          -> rot=0   orient_eff=0   orient_tf=0
  landscape         -> rot=270 orient_eff=90  orient_tf=270
  reverse-landscape -> rot=90  orient_eff=270 orient_tf=90
  reverse-portrait  -> rot=180 orient_eff=180 orient_tf=180
  portrait          -> rot=0   orient_eff=0   orient_tf=0
